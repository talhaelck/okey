## Authored mascots

`scripts/world/mascot_character.gd` builds the seated panda, bunny and dinosaur directly
from original procedural geometry. Their medium-build proportions and green/pink/yellow
hoodies interpret the user-provided character reference image ("ChatGPT Image 8 Tem 2026
13_55_33.png"). No third-party mesh or texture is used for these mascots. The reference
image is not redistributed in this repository; no third-party license is asserted for it.
The CC0 models above remain available as legacy character options.

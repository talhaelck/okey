## Future Features & Expansion Ideas

### Additional Chaos Mechanics
* **Distraction Tools:** Table tapping, lighter flicking, or radio toggle to force opponents to turn their heads.
* **Drink Spilling (Physics):** Knocking over tea/coffee cups to blur or temporarily obstruct adjacent player views.
* **Bomb Tile Mechanic:** A ticking bomb tile that players must secretly reverse-steal into opponents' racks before time runs out.

### Game Modes
* **Blackout Mode:** Periodic room light flickering/outages allowing total dark chaos and blind stealing.
* **Speed/Panic Okey:** Extremely short turn timers forcing high-stress gameplay and mistakes.

### Customization
* Custom hands/gloves (e.g., boxing gloves, cat paws).
* Unlockable room themes (Traditional Coffeehouse, Cyberpunk Bar, Space Station).
